# Abundant Intelligence Lab

> Research led by Karan Girotra at Cornell Tech on how artificial intelligence changes the world and how to deploy and use it to create abundance for everyone.

Cornell Tech, New York, NY. Part of [Cornell Tech](https://tech.cornell.edu/), [Cornell University](https://www.cornell.edu/).

## Mission

We study how artificial intelligence changes the world—and how to deploy and use it to create abundance for everyone.

Research themes: Work, Organizations, Operations, Innovation.

## Founder

**Karan Girotra**, Charles H. Dyson Family Professor of Management at Cornell Tech and the Cornell SC Johnson College of Business.

Karan studies how technology changes work, operations, business models, and the distribution of economic value.

## Research projects

- **AI and Organization Structure**: Research on how artificial intelligence intersects with organizational structure. Team: Hanwen Zhu.
- **Agentic AI Workflows**: Research on agentic artificial intelligence in multi-step workflows. Team: Yiran Fang.
- **Governing Agentic AI Workflows**: Research on governance for agentic artificial intelligence workflows. Team: Lorraine Yuan.
- **Privacy-Preserving Inventory Management**: Research on privacy-preserving approaches to inventory management. Team: Lorraine Yuan.
- **Agentic Shopping**: Research on artificial intelligence agents for shopping. Team: Cecilia Lam, Andrew M. Davis, Evgeny Kagan, Karan Girotra.
- **AI, Organization, and Software Operations**: Research on artificial intelligence as flexible capacity in software production. Team: Bolin Song.
